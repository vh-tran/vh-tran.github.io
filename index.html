Research Interests

  • I study symmetries in neural networks, which boils down to one question: Given a model \(f\), if two parameter settings \(\theta\) and \(\bar{\theta}\) represent the same function, i.e., \(f_{\theta} \equiv f_{\bar{\theta}}\), how are \(\theta\) and \(\bar{\theta}\) related? These symmetries have all sorts of applications, including understanding implicit bias through neural conservation laws, symmetry-aware optimization and quantization, equivariant metanetworks, mode connectivity up to symmetry, and more.

  • Recently, I've also become interested in using AI to tackle math problems, mostly related to commutative algebra and algebraic geometry. Partly for fun and curiosity, but more importantly, as a way to better understand what LLMs can and can't do in mathematical reasoning. I share my thoughts and progress here.

  • Aug 2026. Amid the Math-AI hype, with help from GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Fable 5, and our agentic harness built around these models, we explored several open problems and obtained the following results. All three papers are preprints and have not yet been peer reviewed:

    • The Noetherian case of Bayart's power-series question. If R is a commutative Noetherian ring and R[[x]] is a UFD, then R[[x,y]] is also a UFD. Preprint available here. The question for arbitrary domains, without the Noetherian assumption, remains open.

    • Polynomial extensions of SFT rings. Must R[x] be SFT whenever R is a commutative SFT ring? Positive results were known under additional hypotheses; we answer the general question negatively by constructing a one-dimensional local SFT domain R for which R[x] is not SFT. Preprint available here.

    • A new six-vertex Erdős-Hajnal case. Up to isomorphism, the 26 prime graphs on six vertices form 13 complementary pairs. Four pairs were previously known to have the Erdős-Hajnal property — two through published or accepted results and two through a preprint. We establish a fifth pair by proving the property for the graph with edge set {ab,bc,cd,de,af,bf,df}. Preprint available here.
