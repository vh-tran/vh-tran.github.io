assumptions, scope, and what the animations mean

The laws are exact for continuous-time gradient flow. Finite optimization steps can drift at second order under smoothness assumptions. Architecture animations are schematic level-set motion; experiment traces are simplified visual summaries of the paper’s reported trends.
