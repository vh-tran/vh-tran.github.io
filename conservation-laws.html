The idea

What stays still while everything learns?

Gradient flow gives the instantaneous training motion. A conservation law is a function of the parameters whose value never changes along that motion.

Think of h as a label painted on parameter space: training may travel far, but it cannot cross into a differently labelled surface.

motionθ̇(t) = −∇L𝒟(θ(t))
invarianth(θ(t)) = h(θ(0))

Why the rail holds. The training direction is perpendicular to ∇h, so it is tangent to the current level set. In this model, d(a²−b²)/dt = 0 exactly under gradient flow. Each nonzero hyperbolic level set has separate branches; the characterization says a law is constant on each connected component.

From flow to optimization steps

Exact in continuous time; nearly conserved in steps

What “complete” means here

Exact object being characterized

C¹ functions conserved by Euclidean gradient flow for every dataset and initialization, under a full-span regression-loss assumption. This isolates invariants forced by architecture and optimizer geometry rather than by one dataset.

Gradient flow versus SGD

The laws are exact in continuous time. With a uniformly bounded Hessian for h and a bounded expected squared stochastic-gradient norm, nonlinear laws can accumulate controlled finite-step drift; the affine softmax-router sum remains exact in ideal arithmetic. The experiments use zero weight decay, with full-batch GD and mini-batch SGD testing near-conservation.

Architectural scope

The feedforward and expert results concern single-hidden-layer, bias-free blocks. The page’s MoE animation uses dense softmax gating. The paper states its sparse result for renormalized Top-k softmax with k > 1; the appendix density argument also invokes d > n, although that condition is not listed in the theorem statement.

Attention scope

The characterization covers the paper’s bias-free standalone MHA while its completeness proof varies sequence length. Sinusoidal position encodings are fixed additive inputs. RoPE uses even head dimension and the stated standard non-aliased frequency schedule. Masks, attention biases, learned position encodings, residual paths, LayerNorm, and stacked Transformers are not characterized by these theorems.

Level-set topology

“Determined by an invariant” means constant on each connected component of its level sets. Disconnected branches can carry different values even when the scalar invariant is the same.

Optimization is not free to wander everywhere. The architecture quietly decides which directions are available—and which quantities ideal gradient flow cannot change.
